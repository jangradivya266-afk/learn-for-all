# Learn for All – Free Public Learning Resources

> **"Education Should Be Free for Everyone."**

---

## 📌 Project Overview
* **Project Name:** Learn for All – Free Learning Resources
* **Academic Initiative:** NCU CDOE B.Com Online Community Service Project
* **Nature of Project:** Student-Led Non-Commercial Public Educational Initiative
* **Target Audience:** School students, college learners, competitive exam aspirants, and lifelong self-learners

---

## 🎯 Purpose and Community Impact
The primary objective of **Learn for All** is to democratize education by curating verified, certified, and completely free public educational resources in one accessible portal. 

Students and citizens often spend thousands of rupees on commercial coaching or struggle to navigate disparate government websites. **Learn for All** eliminates this barrier by aggregating:
1. **Government of India Portals:** SWAYAM, NPTEL (IITs/IISc), NCERT, DIKSHA, and ePathshala.
2. **Global Non-Profit Platforms:** Khan Academy, Microsoft Learn, and accredited open YouTube educators.
3. **Original Study Guides:** Topic-wise foundational notes in Accounting, Management, English Grammar, Computer Hardware, and GST.
4. **Interactive Self-Assessment:** Quizzes in Commerce, English, Computer Literacy, and General Knowledge.
5. **Digital Safety Awareness:** Defense against online banking scams, UPI fraud, phishing, and password theft.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Functional Modules

### 1. Subject Categories
* **Commerce and Business:** Financial Accounting, Business Studies, Principles of Management, Business Mathematics, Business Statistics, Economics, and GST Basics.
* **English Learning:** Grammar fundamentals, vocabulary acquisition, spoken English conversations, soft skills, and professional writing.
* **Computer Education:** Computer fundamentals, MS Word, MS Excel, PowerPoint, Internet literacy, and basic programming concepts.
* **School Education:** Free textbooks and curriculum material for Math, Science, Social Studies, and General Knowledge (Classes 1–12).
* **Digital Skills:** Graphic design, digital marketing, Python programming, cybersecurity, and online learning techniques.

### 2. Search & Filter Engine
* Real-time search across resource titles, platforms, and topics.
* Interactive category filter pills with instant count updates.
* Platform dropdown to isolate resources by provider (e.g. SWAYAM, Khan Academy, NCERT).

### 3. Study Materials & Original Notes
* In-app reader for comprehensive study guides with print/save PDF capability.
* Direct links to official NCERT and ePathshala textbook download repositories.

### 4. Interactive Quiz Engine
* 4 Complete Subject Quizzes (Commerce, English, Computer Knowledge, General Knowledge).
* Instant feedback highlighting correct/incorrect choices with detailed conceptual explanations.
* Scorecard with percentage calculation and motivational performance feedback.
* "Try Again" functionality for continuous revision.

### 5. Video Learning Gallery
* Responsive embedded video players for YouTube educational content.
* Watch modal preventing distraction, with quick links to full YouTube playlists.

### 6. Digital Awareness Section
* 6 practical cybersecurity guides covering safe internet browsing, UPI/banking fraud prevention, 2FA, strong passwords, phishing emails, and social media privacy.

### 7. Administrative Management Dashboard
* Password-protected authentication (Default: `learnforall2026`).
* Add new resources with title, category, subcategory, platform, URL, and pricing status.
* Edit existing resources or delete outdated entries.
* **Export JSON Backup:** Download all current resources to a file (`resources.json`).
* **Import JSON Backup:** Upload saved backups to restore or migrate data across devices.
* **Reset to Defaults:** Instant restoration of initial verified database.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, Modern Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+).
* **Design System:** Responsive CSS Custom Properties (Variables), Flexbox, CSS Grid.
* **Icons:** Inline SVG vector graphics (lightweight, zero external font dependencies).
* **Persistence:** Client-side Web Storage API (`localStorage` & `sessionStorage`) with JSON Export/Import engine.
* **Dependencies:** Zero external libraries or paid APIs. Works 100% offline or online.
