About Learn for All

"Learn for All is an independent online community education initiative created to make free learning opportunities accessible to everyone. Our mission is to encourage education, digital literacy and skill development by bringing useful educational resources together in one place. We believe that everyone deserves an opportunity to learn and grow."

This initiative is developed as part of our academic community service curriculum. We do not charge fees, run commercial advertisements, or sell student information. Our objective is to bridge the educational divide by making certified public learning platforms easily discoverable.

🆓 100% Free Access

Education must never be blocked by high price tags. We exclusively catalogue open-access resources, free government portals, and non-profit educational platforms.

🛡️ Zero Commercial Affiliation

This project is purely educational and non-commercial. We maintain no paid partnerships, sponsors, or affiliate commissions with any external portal.

💡 Digital Literacy Mission

Beyond academic theory, our initiative promotes digital safety, banking cyber security, and basic computing skills so every citizen can safely benefit from technology.
